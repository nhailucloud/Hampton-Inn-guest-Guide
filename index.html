Frequently Asked Questions

Common guest questions

Is parking free?

Yes, guest parking is free.

What time is breakfast?

Breakfast is served from 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM.

How do I connect to Wi‑Fi?

Use your last name and room number to log in.

Where can I buy snacks?

7‑Eleven is about 0.2 miles away. Vending machines are also located on the 1st, 3rd, and 4th floors.

Where is the closest grocery store?

Harris Teeter is about 0.3 miles away.

Where is the closest Metro?

Ballston Metro is 2.6 miles away, Pentagon City is 3.1 miles, and Van Dorn Street is 3.2 miles with free parking.

Does the hotel have a shuttle?

No, the hotel does not have a shuttle. Uber and Lyft are recommended.

Where is ice and water?

Ice and water are on the 2nd floor in front of room 213, lobby floor short hallway.

When are cookies available?

Fresh baked cookies are placed out at 5:00 PM.

Is coffee available all day?

Yes, coffee and tea are available 24/7.
